Company profile

One partner for the whole building envelope

Shanghai Wei Gang Architectural & Decoration Engineering Corp. Ltd (上海卫钢建筑装饰工程有限公司), known as WG, was registered in Shanghai in 2009. We are a façade contractor covering design and engineering, material supply, pre-fabrication and installation, so clients can hand us the whole building envelope as one package.

In China our work includes façade packages for the Novartis Shanghai Campus, the CNH campus in Harbin and the Schaeffler campus in Taicang. Since 2018 we have also worked overseas, with two projects in Jamaica and, most recently, the AC Hotel by Marriott in Georgetown, Guyana, completed in 2025. We also supply pre-fabricated windows and doors to customers in Jamaica.

Our own fabrication workshop, running since 2015, lets us build and pre-assemble units and steel frames before they ship, keeping production and quality checks under one roof.

Services

From first drawing to final installation

One contractor from first drawing to final installation. We take on the full building envelope or individual packages.

  1. 01

    Design & engineering

    System design, 3D analysis and shop drawings, including complex geometry like the doubly curved skylight glass at MBJ airport.

  2. 02

    Material supply

    Glass, aluminium profiles, panels and hardware sourced for each project, with samples submitted for approval before production.

  3. 03

    Pre-fabrication

    Unitized panels, frames and steel structures are made and pre-assembled in our workshop, so less is left to do on site.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Our site teams install and glaze in China and overseas, working alongside main contractors such as China Harbour and ZDA Group.
