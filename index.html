From first drawing
to final installation

Four steps, one team. We take on the full building envelope or individual packages.

01

Design & engineering

System design, 3D analysis and shop drawings, including doubly curved skylight glass.

02

Material supply

Glass, profiles, panels and hardware, with samples approved before production.

03

Pre-fabrication

Units and steel frames are built and pre-assembled in our own workshop, running since 2015.

04

Installation

Our site teams install and glaze in China and overseas, alongside main contractors such as China Harbour and ZDA Group.
