About us

Company Profile

Founded in Shanghai in 2010, Shanghai Weigang Building Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd. (上海卫钢建筑装饰工程有限公司) specialises in curtain wall and façade engineering.

In 2015 the company expanded into its own fabrication workshop, bringing production in-house. Since 2023, Weigang has focused on international projects, including the exterior façade of the AC Hotel by Marriott in Guyana.
